🐱 How to Play "Just Divide"

🎯 The Objective

Drag or tap numbered tiles onto the 4×4 grid. Divide and match adjacent tiles to solve numbers and keep the board clear!

✨ Merge Rules

1. Equal Tiles → Both Disappear!

Placing 4 next to 4 causes both to vanish into points.

2. Divisible Tiles → Divide & Merge!

Larger ÷ Smaller must be a whole number.

  • Placing 12 next to 3: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. The 3 disappears and the 12 becomes 4!
  • Placing 15 next to 5 → becomes 3.
  • Placing 9 next to 3 → becomes 3.
3. If Result is 1 → Tile Vanishes!

Division or equal tiles producing 1 disappear completely from the grid.

🎒 Keep & Trash

  • KEEP Slot: Store a tile for later or swap it with your active tile.
  • TRASH Box: Discard unwanted tiles. Uses are limited, but you gain +3 every level!
  • Leveling Up: Every 10 points advances your level.
